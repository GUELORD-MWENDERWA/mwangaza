## Steps

1. Open **Clients**, then **Help requests** (the number next to the name is how many are waiting for you).
2. Open a request: the attached screen, version and phone number are shown. Assign it to someone if needed.
3. Reply: the author gets a notification in the app.
4. Close it once settled.

> **Good to know.** You need the right to handle requests. Real and demonstration modes stay separate.
