## Steps

1. Open **Journal**, then **Help requests**.
2. Open a request: the attached screen and version are shown.
3. Reply: the author gets a notification and an email.
4. Close it once settled.

> **Good to know.** You need the right to handle requests. Real and demonstration modes stay separate.
