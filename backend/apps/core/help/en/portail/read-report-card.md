## Steps

1. Tap your child's name, then **Report cards**.
2. Choose the term.
3. Read the report card or download it as a PDF.

> **Good to know.** The report card shows up only once the school publishes it; it may be withheld if fees are late.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**No report card?** Check with the school: it may not be published yet.
