## Steps

1. On the home page, tap your child's name.
2. Open the payments tab: paid instalments, upcoming ones, balance.
3. Tap a payment to see its receipt.

![A child's payments on the phone.](capture:enfant)

> **Good to know.** To pay, go to the school's cash desk: the parent space does not receive money.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**A payment is missing?** It appears once recorded at the cash desk; keep your paper receipt.
