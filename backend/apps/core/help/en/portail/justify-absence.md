## Your child's attendance

Open the child's page: you see attendance, lateness and absences. You are told when they are absent.

## Justify

1. On the absence, choose **Justify**.
2. Write the reason and attach the certificate (photo or PDF).
3. The school accepts or refuses it; the answer shows on the same page.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**An unreadable certificate?** The school may refuse it: send a new one.
