## Steps

1. Open the address written on the invitation letter.
2. Enter your phone number and the code on the letter.
3. Choose your password.
4. You now see your children.

> **Good to know.** If you have children in several schools, add them with **Add a school**.

## Frequently asked questions

**No letter received?** Ask the school's secretariat for it.
