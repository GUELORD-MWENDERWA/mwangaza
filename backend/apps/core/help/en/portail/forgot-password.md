## Steps

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your phone number.
3. Follow the instructions you receive to choose a new password.

> **Good to know.** If nothing arrives, the school's secretariat can give you a new access letter.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**Phone number changed?** Ask the school to update your record.
