## Steps

1. Open **Grade tracking**, then **Substitutes**.
2. Click **Name a substitute**: choose the course, the person and the reason.
3. Set an end date if you know it; otherwise the substitution lasts until you **remove** it.

> **Good to know.** The substitute sees each student's period total and exam, never the tests. The course shows in their grades marked "substitute". A joint course is substituted for all its classes.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**A sheet already started by the teacher?** It stays closed to the substitute until it is submitted.

**A grade corrected by the substitute?** They must give a reason; the teacher is told and the correction goes to the log.

**Removing the substitute?** They lose access to the course; the grades they entered stay.
