## Steps

1. Open **Marks**, then choose your course and the term.
2. Enter each student's marks; the total is computed for you.
3. Save regularly: the draft is kept, even without internet.
4. When everything is in, click **Send for validation**.

> **Good to know.** A mark cannot exceed the course maximum. After validation, only the management can reopen the sheet.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**Student absent from the test?** Leave the box empty: it is not counted as zero.

**Deadlines?** The management sets a deadline per term; it is shown at the top of the sheet.
