## Steps

1. Open **Fees**.
2. Add a fee (tuition, registration…) and a scale: amount, currency, levels concerned.
3. Choose the schedule: one payment or instalments with their dates.
4. Record the year's exchange rate (dollars and francs).

![Fee scales by level.](capture:frais)

> **Good to know.** Amounts change for the future: what students already paid never moves.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**A discount for one student?** In their record, finances tab, **Grant a discount**.

**The rate changes during the year?** It can be corrected; each payment keeps the rate of the day it was received.
