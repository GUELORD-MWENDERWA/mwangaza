## Issue and print

1. Open **Cards**, tab **Students** or **Staff**.
2. Tick the people (or the whole filtered list), then **Print**: one card per page, or an A4 sheet.
3. A lost card is **revoked**: it is no longer accepted at the entrance, and a new one is issued.

## Official photo

Only the school takes it, with **Photo session** (the device camera, class by class). If the school requires the photo, people without one are left out and counted as “no photo”.

> **Good to know.** A card whose photo, class or name has changed is marked **to reprint**. The **Card model** tab sets the printed fields, the wording and the signature.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**Can a card's QR be recovered?** No: it only exists on the PDF at the moment of issue. For a copy, issue the card again.
