## Record an exception

We start from the published timetable: only what did not go as planned is recorded.

1. Open **Hours worked**, pick the month.
2. **Record an exception**: date, timetable slot, then the type: teacher absent, replaced (pick the substitute), lesson cancelled or extra hour; add the reason.
3. The **Exceptions of the month** tab lists them; an exception can be **cancelled** (it stays in the log).

## Read and print

- **By teacher**: planned, absent, cancelled, replacements done, extra, **worked**.
- **Sheet**: a teacher's PDF for the month; **Export (Excel)**: the whole month.

> **Good to know.** School calendar holidays are not counted. A joint course counts as one hour. The total is recomputed each time, never typed in.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**Who can record?** People with the "Record hours not given" right; the school gives it in **Roles**.

**A mistake?** Cancel the exception and record the right one.
