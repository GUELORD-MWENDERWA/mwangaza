## Steps

1. Open **Expenses**, then **New expense**.
2. Enter the date, category, beneficiary, amount, currency and reason.
3. Choose where the money comes from: cash desk, bank or mobile money.
4. Attach the invoice (photo or PDF, 5 MB at most), then save.
5. Above the school's limit, the expense waits for someone else to **Approve** or **Reject** it.

> **Good to know.** An expense is never deleted: **Cancel** keeps the line, with the reason. The limit and the categories are set at the bottom of the page.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**Can't I approve my own expense?** No: another person with the “Approve an expense” permission must do it.

**Who can see the voucher?** Only the school's staff.
