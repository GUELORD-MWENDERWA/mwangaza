## Prepare the device

1. Connect it to the network once with **Remember this device**: the list of students and cards is kept on the device.
2. Open **Entrance scan** and choose **Camera** (hold the card up) or **Kiosk / ID number** (USB reader, or a typed ID number).

## At each pass

The device decides at once: **Arrived**, **Late** (after the time set by the school), **Already passed** or **Refused** (unknown or revoked card, another school's card, mistyped ID number). The official photo is shown: it must look like the person.

Offline, passes stay on the device and are sent when the network is back, **once each**.

> **Good to know.** Signals (beep, vibration, screen on) are set per device in **This device**. iPhone cannot read QR codes with the camera yet: use the kiosk mode.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**Two devices read the same card?** Only one pass is kept for the day.
