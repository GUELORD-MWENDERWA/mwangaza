## Steps

1. Open the relevant page: **Students** for a class list, the student's record for their form.
2. Click **Print** and choose the options (columns, paper size).
3. The preview opens with your school's logo and letterhead; start the browser's print.

> **Good to know.** The logo, name and header lines come from the **School settings**.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**The logo does not show?** Add it in the school settings.
