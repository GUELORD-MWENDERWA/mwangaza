## Steps

1. Open **Timetable** and set the time grid (days, hours, breaks).
2. Fill in teachers' availabilities and the rooms.
3. Click **Compute**: the app places the lessons and flags conflicts.
4. Adjust by hand if needed, then **Publish**.

> **Good to know.** Once published, each teacher sees their schedule and parents see the class's.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**A conflict remains?** The check names the cause (teacher already busy, room taken); change one of the two.
