## Steps

1. Open the **receipt register** and find the payment.
2. Click **Request cancellation** and write the reason.
3. The authorised person (accountant or management) approves or refuses.
4. Once approved, a cancellation receipt is issued and the student's balance is restored.

> **Good to know.** A payment is never erased: the cancellation is a new linked record, with the reason and the author.
