## Steps

1. Open **Unpaid**.
2. Filter by class, instalment or amount due.
3. Open a student to see their instalments and payments.
4. Print the statements or export the list to Excel.

![The list of unpaid fees by class.](capture:impayes)

> **Good to know.** Late students' parents can be summoned from **Summonses**.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**Which currency are amounts in?** Each debt stays in the fee's currency; the total is converted at the year's rate.
