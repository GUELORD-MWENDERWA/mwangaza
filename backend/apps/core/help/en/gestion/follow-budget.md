## Steps

1. Open **Budget and reports**, pick the year and the currency (one table per currency).
2. Under **Budget**, click **Set the budget**, enter what each category is planned at, then **Save the budget**. Empty or 0 removes the line.
3. Read each category's usage: "Nearly reached" from 90%, "Over budget" above 100%.
4. **Money in and out** shows fees received and expenses paid, month by month.
5. Under **Accounts**, enter the **opening balance** of the bank or Mobile Money, or a **transfer** (for example from the cash desk to the bank).
6. **Excel** downloads the year's workbook (all currencies).

> **Good to know.** An opening balance or transfer is never erased: **Cancel** keeps the line, with the reason. You need the "Manage the budget" permission to set the budget and record movements; the permission to read reports is enough to view them.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**Why no total in dollars and francs?** The two currencies are never added: each has its own table.

**The cash balance doesn't move when I enter an opening balance?** The opening balance is the starting point; the cash desk adds what it receives and subtracts the expenses paid.
