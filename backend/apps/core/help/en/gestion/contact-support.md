## Steps

1. Open **Help**, then **Write to support**.
2. Describe what you wanted to do and what happened.
3. Send: the screen and the app version are attached automatically.
4. Follow the answer in **My requests**; you are also notified.

> **Good to know.** You only see your own requests.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**How many requests per day?** Ten per account; search the help first.
