## Steps

1. Open **Help**, then **Write to support**.
2. Describe what you wanted to do and what happened.
3. Send: the screen and the app version are attached automatically.
4. Follow the answer in **My help requests** (at the bottom of the **Help** panel); you are also notified. You can reply, or close the request with **It's solved**.

> **Good to know.** You only see your own requests. A closed request reopens when you reply, for 30 days. You need a connection to write.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**How many requests per day?** Ten per account; search the help first.
