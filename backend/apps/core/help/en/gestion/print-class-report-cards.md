## Steps

1. Check that every sheet of the term is validated in **Marks validation**.
2. Open **Report cards**, choose the class and the term.
3. Check the preview of one report card (ranks, conduct, signatures).
4. Click **Print the class**: all report cards come out one after another.

> **Good to know.** A student behind on payments does not get the report card, unless the management grants a waiver. The report card follows the school's official format.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**A mark is missing?** The course sheet is not validated yet: remind the teacher.

**Send the report card to parents?** It appears in their space once published.
