## Three helps, always to check

The AI suggests, **you decide**: it never saves anything by itself. You need the "Use AI help" right (given by the school) and credit for the month.

1. **Question** (top bar): "How many 5th A students have unpaid fees?" The answer gives the figures and a link to the screen; it only shows what your rights allow.
2. **AI draft** (announcements and summons): a sentence and a tone give a title and text. Read, edit, then publish yourself.
3. **Read a report card** (Courses and maxima): send a PDF of up to 3 pages or a photo; the grid of courses and maxima fills the screen, correct then **Save**.

> **Good to know.** The AI receives no student name. The uploaded file is deleted right after reading.

## Frequently asked questions

**The button is greyed out?** The reason is written below it: missing right, daily limit or month credit used up.

**A mistake in the grid read?** Correct it on screen before saving; nothing changes before.
