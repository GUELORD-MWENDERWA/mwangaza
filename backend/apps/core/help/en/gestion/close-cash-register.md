## Steps

1. Open **Cash desk**, then **Close the register**.
2. Count the money in dollars and francs and enter the amounts.
3. The app shows the difference with the recorded receipts.
4. If there is no difference, confirm; otherwise write the reason then confirm.
5. Print the closing report.

> **Good to know.** A confirmed closing cannot be changed. Past closings are under **Cash closings**.

## Frequently asked questions

**I forgot to close yesterday?** Close the session left open before opening a new one.
