## Steps

1. Open **Students**, then **Import**.
2. Download the Excel template, fill it in, save it.
3. Upload the file: the app shows a preview and flags the rows to fix.
4. Fix them in Excel and upload again, or confirm the import.

> **Good to know.** An import can be undone: **Cancel this import** removes the students it created, as long as no payment is linked to them.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**Names with accents?** They are kept as they are.

**A row is refused?** The message names the row and the reason (unreadable date, unknown class…).
