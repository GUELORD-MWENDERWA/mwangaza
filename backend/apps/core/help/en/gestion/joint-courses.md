## Create a joint course

1. Open **Courses and maxima**, then **Joint courses**.
2. Choose the level, then **Create a joint course**: tick the classes and the course with the same name.
3. Choose the **group teacher**: they become the teacher of the course in every class.

## Enter the grades of a joint course

1. In **Grade entry**, choose the joint course: the **Joint grade sheet** opens.
2. Students of every class are on one page; each grade goes to the right student and class.
3. Save and submit as for any sheet: each class keeps its own sheet.

> **Good to know.** Each class keeps its maximums, grades and report card. **Removing** a class from the group loses no grade: its course stands alone again.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**The timetable?** A joint course is placed once, at the same time for all its classes.

**Teaching loads?** The hours of a joint course count once for the teacher.
