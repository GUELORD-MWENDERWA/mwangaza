## The day

**Attendance** shows, for the chosen date, who is present, late or absent (including those already justified) and staff present. Tabs: **By class**, **Absences to follow**, **Staff**, **Justifications**, **Correction log**.

## Justifications and corrections

- **Justifications**: the parent sends a note and a certificate from the Portal; you **accept** or **refuse** it.
- **Correcting** a pass needs a reason; the original pass stays, the correction is added to the log.
- **Export (Excel)** gives the day's list.

> **Good to know.** Absences are computed at each opening: enrolled students with no pass or justification, excluding calendar holidays. Parents are told once a day.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**Repeated absences?** Past the threshold set by the school, an alert appears in **Absences to follow**.
