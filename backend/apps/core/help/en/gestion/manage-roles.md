## Steps

1. Open **Staff** to add a person (or import the list from Excel).
2. Choose their role: accountant, cashier, secretary, teacher…
3. Hand them their access letter with the first password.
4. In **Roles**, adjust a role's permissions or create a role of your own.

> **Good to know.** Role changes are written to the audit log.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**Someone leaves the school?** Deactivate their account: their history stays.
