## Steps

1. Open your cash register, then click **Cash out**.
2. Choose the expense to pay: only approved expenses to be paid in cash are listed.
3. Write the name of the person taking the money.
4. Check the expected amount before and after, then click **Cash out and print the voucher**.
5. Have the voucher signed by you and by the person receiving the money.

> **Good to know.** The register cannot pay out more than it received in that currency. At closing, cash outs are deducted from the expected amount and listed on the slip.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**I picked the wrong expense.** While the register is open, a manager can cancel the cash out: the money goes back into the register and the expense becomes payable again.

**Can I do it offline?** Not yet: a cash out needs the network.
