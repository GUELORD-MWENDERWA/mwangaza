## Steps

1. Open **Cash desk** in the menu. If the cash desk is closed, open it first.
2. Find the student by name, middle name or student number.
3. Enter the amount received and choose the currency (dollars or francs).
4. Check the allocation: the amount pays the oldest instalments first.
5. Click **Collect** (F9 key). The receipt prints; hand it to the parent.

![The cash desk: amount entered and allocation across the instalments.](capture:caisse)

> **Good to know.** A payment is never changed or deleted. If there is a mistake, request its cancellation.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**The parent pays in dollars and in francs?** Enter both amounts: the day's rate is printed on the receipt.

**The internet is down?** Collect as usual. The receipt has its number; the payment goes to the server as soon as the internet is back.
