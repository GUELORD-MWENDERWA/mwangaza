## Steps

1. Sign in once with internet, on the device you will use.
2. Open **Prepare the device** in the menu and wait for the download to finish.
3. Work as usual. The sync indicator at the top shows what is waiting to be sent.
4. When internet comes back, sending happens by itself.

> **Good to know.** Payments and marks entered offline are never lost nor counted twice.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**How long does the device last without network?** As long as you like, provided you do not clear the browser data.

**A message asks for my PIN?** Enter it: it protects the device after it stayed offline.
