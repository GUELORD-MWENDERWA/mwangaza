## Steps

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Fill in the name, logo, contact details and the header lines of documents.
3. Choose the currencies, the receipt format and the current school year.
4. In **School organisation**, create the levels, tracks and classes.

> **Good to know.** Nothing is hard-coded: what you enter here appears on receipts, report cards and letters.
