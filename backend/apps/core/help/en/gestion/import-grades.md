## Steps

1. In **Grade entry**, open your course and the period, then **Import from Excel**.
2. **Download the Excel template**: one student per row, one column per assessment, grades already entered included.
3. Fill in the grades in the template, without changing the student number or the name.
4. Choose the workbook, then **Check**: every row is verified.
5. If everything is right, confirm: the grades go into the sheet, as if typed by hand.

> **Good to know.** One wrong row and nothing is saved: fix the workbook and send it again. An empty cell does not change the grade already entered.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**A student not found?** They are recognised by their student number, or by their exact name in the class.

**Without internet?** The check needs the network; saving can wait for the network like any entry.
