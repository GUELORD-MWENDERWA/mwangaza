## Publish the exam timetable

1. Open **Exam timetable** and pick the term.
2. **Add an exam**: class, course, date, time, room, invigilator, type (exam or test).
3. The server checks each addition: a room, an invigilator or a class cannot be booked twice at the same time.
4. When it is right, **Publish**: teachers and families are notified, and the timetable shows in **My timetable** and in the Portal.
5. **Print** gives a class PDF; **General timetable** the whole school's.

> **Good to know.** A change after publishing notifies again. Anyone who turned off timetable notifications gets neither the lesson nor the exam ones.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**Reopen a published timetable?** Yes, **Reopen**, correct, then publish again.

**An invigilator is away?** Change it on the exam's line: pick among teachers who have courses.
