## Steps

1. Open your school's address (the school name is part of it).
2. Enter your phone number and password.
3. If your account uses two-step sign-in, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.
4. On your first sign-in, choose a new password.

> **Good to know.** On a shared computer, sign out when you leave: your name menu, then **Sign out**.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**Forgot your password?** Ask the management or the person who handles accounts to give you a new one.

**Code refused?** Check your phone's clock: it must be exact.
